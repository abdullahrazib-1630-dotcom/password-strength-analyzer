    MODULE 11 — SECURITY GUIDELINES

    Habits that actually keep accounts safe

    01

    Use a unique password everywhere

    Reusing a password means one leaked site compromises every account tied to it.

    02

    Prefer length over complexity

    A 16-character passphrase is harder to crack than an 8-character jumble of symbols.

    03

    Turn on multi-factor authentication

    Even a strong password benefits from a second layer of verification.

    04

    Use a password manager

    Let software generate and remember unique passwords instead of reusing patterns.
